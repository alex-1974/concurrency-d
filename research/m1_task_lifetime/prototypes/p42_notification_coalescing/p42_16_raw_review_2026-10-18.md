# P42.16 — native raw-only archive review (2026-10-18)

**Decision:** bounded P42.16 recorded correctness **PASS**; conditional publication-notify performance promotion **NO-GO**. The existing production pool is unchanged. PR #38 remains Draft; ADR-0001 stays Proposed; no release or performance acceptance is inferred.

## Inputs and evidence level

A user-supplied `raw.tar.gz` was independently inspected (SHA-256 `a918ecffea203f2dd73babd9d42a1eafd967f399f5ee8d093db00abd54e1336c`). It contains exactly 36 per-arm text logs: three sessions, six paired runs each, baseline and conditional. **It does not include** `commit.txt`, native executable/build/source hashes, compiler output, CPU/platform details, clock-unit confirmation or thermal/frequency telemetry. Therefore its numbers **cannot independently be pinned to a source revision, compiled binary or environmental state**. The reference research-code HEAD `b870efad083c18521f7669b4a0d32ee329565e13` passed GitHub Actions [#39063764751](https://github.com/alex-1974/concurrency-d/actions/runs/39063764751) on DMD 2.111.0 and LDC 1.41.0; that code gate does not fill the missing *native-run provenance*.

Every uploaded file has 517 lines: 444 individual task records, 36 distinct round-level waiter snapshots, 36 round summaries and one PASS marker. All 36 files, 15,984 task records and 1,296 rounds parsed without duplicates, missing IDs, invalid workers, negative or inconsistent per-task timestamps, or worker distribution mismatches. In each round the recorded aggregate enrollment was 4 and each of the four worker-specific enrolled flags was true with at least one wait entry (in fact, all entry vectors were `1,1,1,1`). The intended all-task-complete-before-close property is represented by the consumer's PASS marker, not independently proved from raw text alone. Baseline preceded conditional in 9 of 18 pairs.

## Comparison — raw MonoTime ticks, no unit conversion

Individual-task publication-to-start latency is **not** isolated kernel wake time. Arm medians pool task observations, whereas the paired delta first computes each arm's task-latency median *within a session/pair/shape*, subtracts baseline from conditional, and finally takes the median of 18 differences.

| Tasks/round | Baseline pooled median | Conditional pooled median | Median paired delta (cond − base) | Conditional faster pairs |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5,016 | 5,145 | +117.5 | 8/18 |
| 4 | 6,065 | 6,154.5 | +266.25 | 7/18 |
| 32 | 5,419.5 | 5,211.5 | −18.5 | 9/18 |

For 32-task work, descriptive pooled individual-task P95/P99 are 24,380/41,643 for baseline versus 27,399/49,122 for conditional. Higher pooled tails under conditional are a **follow-up signal**, not a statistically qualified regression because within-run tasks are correlated and the number of independent sessions is small.

Actual execution distribution in 216 rounds per arm/shape:

| Tasks/round | Arm | 1 active worker | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | Baseline | 138 | 66 | 12 | 0 |
| 4 | Conditional | 131 | 77 | 6 | 2 |
| 32 | Baseline | 8 | 7 | 44 | 157 |
| 32 | Conditional | 3 | 11 | 56 | 146 |

All one-task rounds necessarily have one executing worker (216/216 in each arm). Four simultaneous condition-wait enrollments do not prove four OS-level kernel sleepers or tell which workers received a notification without eventually executing a task.

## Drift and decision

Session 3 pair 5 strongly favors conditional across all three task shapes (one-task median: baseline 50,006 ticks versus conditional 7,983.5); the following pair 6 reverses the direction (baseline 9,282 versus conditional 78,636.5). The cause of this large cross-shape run variation is **unknown**, not evidence of notification-policy effects. The near-zero paired median deltas, approximately split signs, and nonuniform tails do **not** qualify a speed-up, fairness improvement or starvation-freedom claim.

**KEEP** this finite-run, structurally valid observation evidence. **NO-GO** for merging notification suppression into production or treating PR #38 as an accepted lifetime design.

**Next evidence gate:** recover the complete native XPS output directory (source/binary hashes, exact compiler/build, platform and timestamp/thermal/frequency telemetry). Retain immutable raw logs under the SHA above. Analyze paired run-level and session-level drift before considering further instrumented wake/claim/handoff tracing. Separately retain a semantically matched *uninstrumented* optimized end-to-end benchmark for any throughput claim. Do not promote either test style as the other.

The full independent local extraction contains 15,984-row task CSV, 1,296-round CSV and 54-row paired-run CSV. These are derived data, not new native measurements.
