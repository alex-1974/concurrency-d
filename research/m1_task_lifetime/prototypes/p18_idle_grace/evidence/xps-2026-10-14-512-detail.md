**Interpretation:** 1- and 2-worker regressions persist for all three ingress capacities in pooled medians and are visible in sessions 2–4. However, session 1 favors P18 for both worker counts. This session dependence warns against claiming a deterministic cost of the extra idle search or ignoring frequency drift, startup behavior and system noise. Even at 512 iterations the workload still includes producer backpressure, Condition parking, 2,048 TaskRefs × 3 waves and per-wave polling. The counterexamples do **not** establish that longer task computations themselves are slower.
